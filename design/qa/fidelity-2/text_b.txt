1.  Abstract
Launch an Android-installable PWA for Shubh Vastu Consultancy, with Marathi as the default and Hindi and English support. React provides the customer interface; a Firebase backend controls identity, quotas, payments and access to AstrologyAPI.
Phase 1 includes birth charts, AI Guruji, basic Vastu, a gated Prashna workflow and prepaid memberships. Reliable provider data is required for every chart interpretation. The design targets an initial pilot of 100 paying users; this is a planning scenario, not a capacity guarantee.

3.  Background and Problem Statement
Customers need accessible regional-language guidance and a clear route to Dr. Jagdish Mohan Mavlankar. The product must distinguish traditional interpretations from practical advice and make subscription limits visible before customers pay.
The client owns the source code, domain and service accounts. The PWA cannot call paid providers directly. The backend is the sole authority for paid entitlements and calculation provenance. A full paid MVP exceeds the original INR 10,000 budget; reduce scope if that ceiling remains fixed.

5.  Request Lifecycle
A signed-in customer submits a birth profile, question or Vastu assessment with a unique request ID. The interface shows the relevant allowance before submission.
The backend verifies the Firebase ID token, resource ownership, payload limits and rate limits. It normalizes birth time, location, timezone and calculation settings.
Load the server-owned plan version, active period, quota counters and consent state. Reuse valid cached natal data only when the complete calculation key matches.
Atomically reserve the required allowance and create a pending operation. Call the selected provider adapter outside the Firestore transaction; never make external calls inside a retried transaction.
Persist provider responses and provenance before marking the operation complete. AI receives verified chart facts; missing facts produce a clarification or unavailable state, not guessed positions.
Use explicit provider deadlines. Retry only safe reads with bounded backoff. A timed-out chat or payment call is uncertain; reconcile it rather than blindly issuing duplicate billable work.
Commit usage once on successful delivery; release the reservation on confirmed failure. Return the saved response and remaining quota. Log request IDs, latency and costs without birth details or message bodies.

7. Consistency, Idempotency, and Replay
Firestore transactions serialize entitlement changes and quota reservations. External provider execution cannot be made exactly once without vendor support. A durable operation record allows pending and uncertain attempts to be reconciled without charging the customer twice.

8. Security and Privacy Considerations
Use Firebase Authentication with Google sign-in in the MVP. Firestore rules restrict users to their own profiles and saved results. Deny direct writes to payments, entitlements, usage and operations.
Collect only necessary birth and property information with clear consent. Do not place birth details, questions or access tokens in URLs, analytics or routine logs. Keep sensitive responses out of service-worker caches.
Store provider keys and webhook secrets in Secret Manager. Use separate development and production projects, least-privilege service identities and server-only credentials.
Require privileged roles for support tools. Keep provider calls and quota mutations behind authenticated routes; add per-user limits and App Check where supported. Disable sensitive debug logging in production.
Offer deletion of profiles and saved conversations. Propose 90-day chat retention and profile retention until deletion, subject to client approval. Keep legally required payment records separately; verify provider retention and region availability before launch.

The prototype is a local interactive design, not a live service. It demonstrates all seven screens in Marathi, Hindi and English. Forms show a safe provider-unavailable state; no astrological results, payments or authentication are simulated as successful.

Birth profile: local date and exact time, selected place label, latitude, longitude, IANA timezone, UTC instant and time certainty. Cache keys include normalized inputs, ayanamsa, chart settings, provider and calculation version. Natal data can be reused; transits and Panchang require date-sensitive keys and expiry.
AstrologyAPI adapter: geo_details, timezone_with_dst, astro_details, planets, horo_chart/:chart_id, major_vdasha and current_vdasha form the proposed chart bundle. Guruji uses hosted /api/chat with Standard or Advanced level. Basic Vastu uses score, findings and remedies. Confirm exact routes, auth, response shapes and language support in M1.
Payment flow: create order, complete checkout, show pending, receive signed webhook, verify captured payment and matching amount/currency, then activate a 30-day period atomically. Do not activate from a browser success callback. For MVP, prohibit overlapping purchases; refunds and disputes require recorded entitlement adjustments.
